Our Story & Craft

A Quiet Celebration of Nigerian Terroir

Born from a desire to honour Nigeria's culinary depth with unhurried elegance, The Olive Table pairs time-honoured regional recipes with precision open-fire cooking and curated Mediterranean olive infusions.

Every morning, our kitchen receives vine-ripened peppers from Kano, sweet plantains from Ondo, organic highland greens from Jos, and line-caught croaker flown fresh from the Lagos coast. In our serene olive-and-limestone dining room, every plate tells a story of warmth, memory, and refinement.

Farm & Coast Sourcing
